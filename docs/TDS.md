## 1. Runtime ownership

OWNER-T02 approves one authoritative simulation per run, in the solo process or on the cooperative host. Use typed GDScript for rules and Godot scenes for authored layouts, collision geometry, animation and controls. The same action submitted from the local keyboard, a network peer or an agent follows the same validation and commitment path. A run starts with an explicit ruleset, catalog revision, seed, selected challenge configuration and initialized party roster.

| Owner | Writable state and responsibility | Inputs and published results |
|---|---|---|
| Content catalog | Validated, read-only definitions and stable references | Supplies definitions to simulation, preparation, Codex and presentation |
| Run session | Run clock, phase, party scaling baseline, player identity and terminal result | Orders commands and simulation work, coordinates world transition and results |
| World and encounters | Region/realm topology, spatial occupancy, faction actors, bodies, encounter membership, objectives, finite stock and exploration | Accepts movement, interactions and resolved destruction. Supplies candidate geometry and eligible world facts |
| Combat | Committed actions, deliveries, snapshots, statuses, timers, barriers, entity combat lifecycles and causal resolution | Accepts intents and contact facts. Publishes attributed outcomes, including elimination and cleanup reasons |
| Run progression | Shared XP/level, individual builds, frozen rewards, wallets, catalyst eligibility and run-scoped benefits | Consumes authorized outcomes once. Emits individual selections, agreed purchases and permanent award instructions |
| Local profile store | Durable personal entitlements, balances, unlocks, Codex, quests, upgrades, mastery and settings | Applies validated transactions and returns a receipt. Provides a versioned preparation view |
| Input, transport and views | Bindings, peer connection, displayed state, interpolation, audio and visual effects | Submit intentions and display permitted results. Presentation cannot confirm a hit, grant an item or reveal hidden geography |

Use explicit typed calls for decisions with a result, such as a cost validation. Queue causally related work under the authority. Use signals for notifications to views after a state change. Scene signal arrival order, node paths, animation callbacks and network packet order are unsuitable tie-breakers for gameplay. Give transient instances a run-scoped identity and lifecycle number. Distinguish those from catalog identity, player identity, connection ID and the source family used for reaction eligibility.

Keep inspectable nodes at scene boundaries. Put calculations that need no scene tree in small typed objects/functions. Per-run services belong to the run and are released together. Process-wide ownership is appropriate for the loaded catalog and access to the current local profile. This limits accidental state carryover when returning to preparation. Use a single writer for each mutable aggregate. Profile I/O receives immutable transaction data so an incomplete write cannot mutate a live scene.

## 2. Combat resolution

The following contracts realize **Action, Delivery, Payload and Trigger**. Several attacks may reuse one delivery or one damage rule. An attack with an unusual shape can implement that shape directly. The four responsibilities need no parallel inheritance hierarchy. [GDS §§2–4](GDS.md#gds-combat)

| Responsibility | Inputs and commitments | Inspectable result |
|---|---|---|
| Action | Validate owner state, target/placement, cooldown, mandatory resources and charges together. Commit costs at their declared scope. Capture offense, source, eligible inherited properties, aim and offensive modifiers | Accepted activation or a reason for rejection, costs paid, snapshot ID and timing |
| Delivery | Place, sweep or move the actual geometry in its region/realm. Resolve obstruction, interception, height/depth, spectral eligibility and contact interval | Time and position of genuine contact, or a recorded miss, interception or incompatible target |
| Payload | Resolve each typed component against live target defense, resistance and vulnerability. Apply eligible resource, status or force effects only at their stage | Component accounting, barrier layers consumed, HP removed, overkill, actual displacement and cause |
| Trigger | Match a specific event, owner, source family, target, conditions and eligible continuation. Reserve a charge/status once, then create a child where authorized | Parent/child IDs, rule identity, consumption, timing and reason for firing or rejecting |

**Activation and contact.** Automatic attacks normally wait for an eligible target. Authored orbit, placement, channel and other explicit exceptions follow their own activation rules. Rebindable manual actions validate their own placement. A valid miss pays the committed cost. Invalid placement spends nothing. An activation-priced sweep pays once across its targets. A separately priced follow-up can fail without refunding its parent. HP costs bypass barriers, record actual HP paid and can kill the caster, ending the affected cast. Preserve the distinct successful-attack condition for Bloom Reserve and the protection rule for fatal automatic HP costs during revival. [GDS §§2.2, 3.6, 5.4](GDS.md#gds-combat)

Use a spatial candidate search, followed by exact delivery geometry and eligibility. Test a fast projectile over its traveled segment, a sweep over its arc, a beam against its clipped line and an enclosed tether against the actual unobstructed triangle. Include starting overlap when applicable. A directed shield can intercept before damage reaches a creature. Ordinary crowd overlap permits movement and uses the authored shared contact-damage interval. It does not generate repeated impacts. A Bow can miss a moving target, a Chakram returns toward its moving owner, and Arcane Focus retains its committed direction as its origin moves. Movement, aim direction, prediction, guidance and projectile speed have independent inputs. [GDS §§2, 7–8, 10](GDS.md#gds-controls)

<a id="tds-space"></a>

### Authoritative geometry (F-01)

The world's realm-local spatial service is the **only writer** of gameplay pose, velocity, shape, occupancy and contact history, including players, enemies, moving shields, doors, constructs, destructibles and projectiles. Combat requests a synchronous change from this service. Navigation reads its versioned state. Godot is the authoring surface for static terrain and collision outlines. At admission, convert validated outlines and module transforms into immutable realm-space obstruction primitives. A destructible or moving part is represented in the mutable set from the start. Godot nodes mirror committed poses for rendering, editing and debug overlays. Physics step callbacks, Area overlap lists and interpolated client transforms cannot produce a second gameplay contact.

Each realm has a broad-phase index containing complete dynamic bounds and an index of its static polygons/segments. Insert objects into every touched cell or equivalent tree extent, including across module seams. Enumerate every member of a visited bucket even when all occupants share one coordinate. Deduplicate proxies, preserve distinct eligible subshapes/occurrences, then filter by compatibility and perform the actual swept/overlap test. Clip rays, beams and links to eligible blockers. Test initial overlaps, melee arcs, moving targets, returning projectiles, thin obstacles and field intervals. Ordinary crowds can overlap. Detection and displacement resistance are separate decisions. Exhausted scratch capacity grows or streams the full candidate set. The independent reference is brute-force enumeration on the same geometry. Godot's bounded shape queries are useful for diagnostic comparison. [Godot 4.7 PhysicsDirectSpaceState2D](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/classes/class_physicsdirectspacestate2d.html)

At the displacement stage resolve the swept motion against the current realm, update the canonical pose and index **synchronously**, then produce impact facts. Child collisions and subsequent deliveries see the new pose, including after a construct breaks. Already admitted simultaneous contacts keep their captured facts. When several impulses share a target, aggregate their directed contributions under the ownership rule before movement. Keep geometry, broad-phase candidates and accepted contacts inspectable at the same logical boundary. A spatial subdivision or a larger engine query limit alone cannot prove completeness.

**Accounting.** Keep activation offense immutable, including inherited weapon costs/drawbacks. Deduplicate a rune by qualifying attack instance and modifier identity. A DoT takes its offensive snapshot at application and samples live target defense on each eligible tick. For each physical/magical component use GDS §3.5 category stacking, target defense reduction, attack-local penetration, mitigation and post-mitigation vulnerability in order. Track resolved incoming amount, per-barrier absorption, actual HP loss and excess overkill separately. An accepted hit can deal zero damage. Successful damage requires positive barrier or HP damage. Recovery based on damage reads only eligible owned hostile HP actually removed. [GDS §§3.2–3.6](GDS.md#gds-combat)

**What an attack can change (F-06).** At commitment record inherited offense, rune permissions, costs and base geometry parameters. A persistent or traveling instance owns separate mutable counters. Pressure Charge reads its mine's armed waiting time. Ascending Pierce increases only for later penetrations by that spear. Rebound Momentum advances on a genuine rebound. Resonant Current tracks consecutive compatible contacts with each target. Outer Edge compares actual contact with otherwise applicable geometry, and Expanding Domain changes actual coverage over its lifetime. A Seismic mine wave may charge an already-flying shard and the shard consumes that charge on its next genuine rebound. These transitions do not resample owner buffs. An attack-applied DoT starts its own lifecycle using the parent's inherited offensive snapshot and any explicitly eligible instance-local value at the application, then samples live target defense on each tick. Preserve source-specific cadence, ownership and refresh comparators. [GDS §§3.2, 3.8, 9–10](GDS.md#gds-powers)

**Personal survival state.** Absorb through compatible temporary barriers by remaining lifetime, oldest first on ties, then persistent layers by default. Store source, lifetime, capacity, generation and any fortification charge per layer. Apply each source's accumulate/replace/decay rule. Clamping current HP after a max-HP change, expiring a barrier or replacing it carries its own reason. Only hostile depletion of the associated fortified layer qualifies for Aegis Release. Track limited recovery, amplification once, actual HP restored and eligible excess separately. Only the permitted excess enters Crimson Reservoir. Keep conversion lineage so a release may heal missing HP without refilling its own reserve. A larger cap alone adds no HP or barrier. [GDS §§4.2–4.4](GDS.md#gds-survival) · [Sanguine Aegis](GDS.md#catalog-f06) Compare whole eligible barrier instances by remaining capacity, then longer remaining lifetime on a capacity tie. For a strongest-replacement source, 80 for 5 s beats 60 for 8 s. A new 80 for 8 s wins against 80 for 5 s. Preserve the winner's own capacity, life and source.

<a id="tds-groups"></a>

### Group membership and resolution (F-02)

Give each origin a due instant in authoritative logical time. Include the ordered sub-tick time of impact for swept delivery. Distinct travel times, scheduled pulses and attacks committed in sequence retain that order. Resolve the earliest due instant first. Independent accepted strikes with the same due instant and no causal ordering, and the contacts of an instantaneous multi-target area, form the root wave. A traveling wave can reach its targets at different instants. Use a documented common time representation and refine ambiguous swept times before declaring simultaneity. Sharing a physics/render frame or falling within a convenient frame epsilon is insufficient.

Freeze the eligible committed roots, contact set, relevant target conditions and defensive state before resolving that wave. Work breadth-first across its targets through the stages below. A root committed and admitted while alive cannot be removed because a different root kills its owner at the same instant. A dead actor cannot commit a new ordinary action. Allocate finite barrier/HP debits once per target, mark immediate zero-HP transitions, and retain origin-specific accepted damage and depletion facts for reactions. Use authored priorities and the recorded contention rule PR12 only for genuinely shared finite claims. Node/peer IDs, spawn insertion, bucket traversal and packet arrival cannot arbitrate victory. [Provisional contention rule](#tds-rules)

| GDS stage | What the authority records or does |
|---|---|
| 1 · Before impact | Capture the pre-existing target statuses and eligibility facts required by this origin. |
| 2 · Originating damage | Finish component and barrier/HP accounting. Preserve the facts needed by later stages. |
| 3 · Lethality | Mark immediate death and once-per-life elimination with its primary owner. Preserve qualifying death-state facts. |
| 4 · Hit, statuses and rune reactions | Check source permission and captured state. Recheck live finite resources at consumption using authored priority and a stable tie-breaker. A dead target rejects ordinary new statuses. |
| 5 · Displacement and impacts | Move only eligible living targets, resolve solids and derive qualifying new physical contacts. |
| 6 · Recovery, resources and defense | Resolve eligible restoration/conversion. A barrier generated here applies to subsequent incoming damage. Healing cannot reverse a finalized death. |
| 7 · Elimination effects and entitlement | Dispatch eligible on-kill/assist effects and progression claims with distinct identities and scopes. |
| 8 · Children | Give each new event its own applicable cycle. Drain eligible immediate descendants before terminal arbitration. Leave future scheduled ticks/projectiles outside the completed group. |

All immediate children carry the group's instant, parent and a new wave number. Execute the next wave after the preceding wave completes its stages. Fresh child contacts use the updated geometry and the applicable pre-state rule. New statuses cannot satisfy their originating hit. Revalidate any shared finite claim. An explicitly delayed Echo, a future DoT tick and a projectile with remaining travel belong to later instants even when the renderer draws both together. Preserve the attack family distinction for Converging Strikes. Two Familiar darts remain one family.

A lethal hostile hit can admit immediate Reprisal Pulse. Hostile depletion of the associated fortified layer can separately admit one Aegis Release, including on a lethal hit. These descendants use the recorded owner and facts. Neither permits the dead controller to act, heals the dead, or lets the release recharge its reserve/barrier. Once every eligible immediate wave drains, seal one terminal decision. Both last survivor and Creator dead gives victory. A surviving Creator with no eligible survivor gives defeat. Reversing scene insertion, numerical IDs, target traversal or root enqueue order must preserve the result and receipts. [GDS §§3.4, 4.5](GDS.md#gds-survival)

**Timing and continuation.** Distinguish cooldown, attack interval, orbit speed, per-target interval, DoT cadence and lifetime. Retain accrued progress across rate changes and transformations. Refresh eligible statuses without restarting the tick clock. A tick at natural expiry resolves before a single eligible expiry reaction. Exit/reentry does not grant another immediate field tick. Keep owner-specific Bleed/Burn, strongest effective Chill, capped aggregate Fracture and owner-specific markers under their own rules. Capture the end reason, such as natural expiry, hostile depletion, replacement, destruction or realm cleanup. [GDS §§3.7–3.8, 8.1](GDS.md#gds-combat)

**Progress and failure.** Each generating rule declares what makes a successor distinct and what is spent, advances or terminates it. Examples include a new elimination, a consumed mine, diminishing resource, distinct contact or advancing schedule. For a cyclic set of immediate rules, require a finite decreasing measure, a new eligible lifecycle/contact from a finite set, or a genuine future deadline on every repeatable path. Static checks reject unconditionally self-enabling zero-cost cycles. During play, detect a repeated equivalent causal state in the same ancestry with identical future eligibility and no resource, target or time progress. Exclude incidental event serial numbers from this comparison. Distinct targets, independent siblings and valid chains at their amplification cap remain eligible. On a proven invalid cycle, quarantine the uncommitted group, retain a local reproducible trace and surface a simulation fault. Recover from a verified closed boundary only if safe, otherwise end as a technical interruption and reconcile durable rewards. Never publish a partial outcome or silently trim a finite chain.

**Across rendered frames.** A work slice may yield between recorded stage/wave cursors. Logical time, authoritative movement/physics, spawn clocks, tick timers, terminal arbitration and subsequent groups wait at the same frontier. Buffer bounded sequenced input for the next legal instant, coalesce superseded movement and explicitly reject an expired discrete command. Never insert late inputs into an admitted wave. Keep sockets, heartbeats, real-time host-recovery deadlines, UI and last committed presentation responsive. Publish only the closed result. Trace group age, cursor, queue, memory, work yielded and input age. A slow finite group is a performance finding, not proof of an invalid loop. A real resource failure must be visible and preserve the last consistent settlement.

**Physical ownership.** Record contributing impulses with direction, source, attenuation and remaining influence. Derive impact from relative normal motion and effective mass/resistance. Credit the strongest remaining contribution in the actual impact direction using a stable tie-breaker. Keep secondary participation separately. Expire influence with motion. A resting contact requires separation and a new impact before another collision reaction. A faction-triggered crystal or neutral hazard can kill without creating a player claim. Authored force can add energy. Document O23–O26 for ambiguous contacts and hazards. [GDS §§5.3, 16](GDS.md#gds-environment)

**Death and outcomes.** Store the owner's persistent combat identity separately from the owner's active controller. While another eligible survivor sustains the run, committed projectiles, mines, statuses, echoes and valid fields can finish with original attribution. Orbit damage and new Familiar/Guardian attacks suspend. A post-death owned Bleed kill may trigger Death Bloom. A newly selected spectating reward records acquisition immediately and starts active behavior only on revival. Resolve one terminal result after the current simultaneous group. Mutual last-survivor/Creator death in that group wins. If the Creator survives the wipe, future scheduled attacks cannot postpone defeat. Terminal cleanup supplies no fake expirations or kills. [GDS §§4.5–4.6](GDS.md#gds-survival)

### Transformation and payment contracts (F-13)

A catalog entry declares the catalyst capability a form offers to other recipes, independent of base-slot occupancy. Mark each capability as retained, transformed or removed. Evaluate eligibility against the current build at earn and again at selection. Transform in one operation with the previous form's ownership, charges, contact clocks and active-instance list. New commitments use the new form. Already launched attacks, statuses and armed effects finish under their original snapshot, source and lifetime unless an authored transfer explicitly transforms that instance once. No retroactive reroll, counter reset, free summon or double activation.

- **Thermal Equilibrium.** Equip Flame and Frost. Lingering Essence 5 with Flame can acquire Eternal Ember, and Arcane Tether 5 with Frost can acquire Binding Circuit, in either order. Once both are selected a later reward may offer F03, even to an owner with Unknown recipe knowledge. Preserve actual links and stationary anchor geometry for an isolated target. Thermal Shock transmission deals damage along compatible unobstructed links. Only Eternal Ember's qualifying heat pulse transfers bounded Burn to Chilled enemies actually reached. Apply PR10's owner-specific comparator. Keep the source Burn, Chill timing and independent Thermal Shock cooldown intact. [GDS F03](GDS.md#catalog-f03)
- **Sanguine Aegis.** Vital Exchange 5 needs Reprisal Pulse ownership for Crimson Reservoir. Reprisal Pulse 5 needs Vital Exchange ownership for Mirror Guard. Both base powers are obtainable first. Each evolved form explicitly retains the base catalyst capability needed for the other, so either selection order works. An occupied base slot without that declared capability fails validation. A later selection combines the two slots. Fortify only a valid barrier with real reserve, and charge/release from its own absorbed hostile damage. [GDS F06](GDS.md#catalog-f06)
- **Delivery exceptions.** An authored precision mechanism may redirect the next eligible automatic attack to a discovered object. Submit it through ordinary targeting, payment, range, obstruction and timing. Dangerous objects take genuine incidental hits, and undiscovered secrets stay hidden. Declare per-activation, channel-interval, pulse, explicitly per-target and child costs separately. Validate resources before each newly priced interval. An unaffordable interval supplies neither free delivery nor a refund of earlier commitments. [GDS §§2.2, 3.6](GDS.md#gds-controls)

## 4. World and encounters

Generate a connected graph of authored modules with travel links, validated entrances, collision geometry and separately seeded placement/population streams. Store generation revision, seed, selected modules, transforms, links and stable instance IDs. Three regional cores and mixed transitions support loops, alternate routes and backtracking. Guarantee Ancient Worldtree, Ancient Foundry and Fallen Citadel at generation. The other nine landmark types are variable. Test reachability in the graph and clearance in the realized collision geometry with ordinary starter movement. A graph edge alone cannot establish that a body or realm exit can be reached. [GDS §§11, 16](GDS.md#gds-world)

Give the overworld and each optional realm its own spatial identity. Realm-local collision prevents cross-dimension hits. The run owns time and persists destructibles, shortcuts, deaths/entitlements, active trials, vendor stock, bodies, pending effects and exploration across view unloads. Rebuild views from authoritative state on return.

<a id="tds-nav"></a>

### Navigation and offscreen activity (F-08)

The world owns a realm-local walkability grid/graph derived from the same obstacle shapes used by combat. Include swept actor clearance, traversal permissions, dynamic occupancy revisions, portals and both ends of a shortcut. Ordinary crowds may overlap. Routes must work for baseline voluntary movement. Enemy pathfinding uses the graph to approach a sensed target, then the spatial authority resolves actual motion/obstruction. Hunters use authored sensing, last-known position and permitted cross-region edges. A known player coordinate from the networking layer cannot guide an undetected Hunter across a realm boundary.

At generation prove core, guaranteed landmark and gateway connectivity and a viable exit for each realm. Reserve clearance corridors or alternative routes to essential exits and current corpses. Stage footprint changes from constructs, Living Geometry, destructibles, hazards or moved objects. Check affected components with incremental flood-fill/reachability against the authoritative shape version, including navigable clearance and time to escape warned hazards. If a critical edge is uncertain, run a full realm/path check before publishing. Reject or adjust an invalid placement/solidification under its placement rules, or clamp a moving obstruction to the last legal pose. Maintain physical route existence without revealing the route or promising safe survival.

Use full spatial/contact resolution wherever actors can affect each other, including unwatched faction battles, roaming Hunters, moving projectiles and occupied split realms. For a distant area with no interactions in progress, retain its next due spawn, travel crossing, AI decision, event deadline, status/timer or other consequential change on the global logical scheduler. Wake it before that instant or before an actor can enter its influence. Only a region with no actors, pending interactions or due work can sleep until its next scheduled change. Preserve RNG consumption, faction outcomes, stock, elapsed effects and fog independently of camera and view unloading. Reject approximation if equivalence to full advancement has not been demonstrated. Compare an observed and an unobserved sister run under identical accepted inputs.

Fog is a shared record of physically discovered geography. Visibility of actors, hidden locations, personal records and spectator views is a separate filter. NPC travel and projectiles cannot reveal fog. Optional realms have usable return routes, independent entrances/exploration, persistent bodies and ongoing Collapse pressure. Preserve completed mechanisms and shortcuts. A remote teammate leaving cannot reset a still-occupied puzzle. PR11 supplies the prototype abandonment and dormant-trial rule for O33–O34. [GDS §§5, 11, 17–18](GDS.md#gds-world)

Model enemies with authored sensing, faction relationships, preparation, committed attacks and recovery. Elites add their named behavior to the base, bosses keep highest reached phases while allowing genuine burst kills, and reinforcements use independent origin and reward categories. Hunters prioritize detected living players and can engage factions or lose pursuit. Erasure arrives only in Collapse. A new Hunter changes population and grants its own authored rewards on a qualifying kill. Repeated fresh kills keep full rewards. The final Creator's temporary attack echoes and four independently damageable manifestations require different lifecycles. [GDS §§12–15](GDS.md#gds-enemies)

Objects, physical momentum, seven hazards and eight mechanism types reuse the combat/contact rules. Give damaged terrain, finite pickups, exhausted stations and NPC stock stable owners. Recheck essential paths when movable or newly created solids, Living Geometry or Collapse hazards could seal a passage. Reserve physical exits and body-rescue access without revealing their route. An occupied plate can respond to eligible mass and continued contact without continuously issuing completion rewards. Shared stock and the buyer's personal payment commit together within the run. [GDS §§16–19](GDS.md#gds-environment)

At 25:00 the authoritative clock starts Collapse, reveals the final gateway and major connections, keeps minor fog and closes new optional starts. Existing events, services, combat drops, Hunter arrivals and valid exits follow their own rules. One living entrant at a valid normal or authored early gateway commits a single irreversible transition for the party and bodies across all realms. Carry individual builds, pending choices, applicable effects, death counts and waits. Revoke old-world commands and stop overworld threats with a cleanup reason. Snapshot destination state for slow or reconnecting clients. No vote, automatic revival or resource refill. Apply disclosed event outcomes, safe preparation and subsequent Creator activation. ## 5. Cooperative sessions

Authority, transport and host-loss policy are separate decisions. Compare the following options against split-realm play, durable rewards and the owner's limited operations role.

| Approach | Benefit | Cost or unresolved dependency | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Player-hosted authoritative session, native ENet | One simulation decides contacts, world changes and awards. Solo can use the same command handler locally. No standing service | Host CPU/uplink covers all active realms. Direct Internet play needs a reachable host. Host failure interrupts authority | Single authority and prototype LAN/direct-IP scope approved by OWNER-T02. ENet adapter proposed |
| Deterministic peer lockstep | Modest input bandwidth and potential command replay | Every peer must agree on physics contacts, ordering, RNG and numeric results across versions. Slow/lost peers affect play. Authority for rewards and recovery still needs a policy | Defer. The engine and toolchain evidence provides no cross-machine determinism guarantee |
| Dedicated authoritative server or mandatory relay | Dedicated hosting can survive a player's departure. A relay can improve reachability while retaining a player host | Hosting, credentials, deployment and recurring operations need ownership/funding. A relay alone cannot recover a failed host's simulation | Requires a separate product/operational decision |
| Authority with WebRTC or WebSocket transport | Potential browser connectivity while reusing commands and snapshots | WebRTC needs signaling and may need relay coverage. WebSocket uses an ordered reliable stream with different latency behavior. Native/browser interoperation needs proof | Keep transport replaceable. Browser multiplayer and its infrastructure remain unapproved |

**Protocol and transport (F-07).** Keep six boundaries explicit. Simulation determines facts. A transport-independent command schema expresses player intentions. An encoder publishes permitted state. A transport adapter carries bytes. Discovery supplies a host address or a manually entered direct IP. Public Internet reachability depends on routing/NAT and is outside this prototype gate. Solo uses the same validated command handler locally. Native ENet is the first candidate. WebRTC or WebSocket could carry a compatible protocol after separate verification. Godot's scene replication is convenient for shared node trees, but implicit node lifecycles/properties complicate realm relevance, fog privacy, snapshot replay and headless protocol tests. Prefer explicit versioned command and snapshot messages. [Godot 4.7 multiplayer](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/networking/high_level_multiplayer.html)

Separate replaceable sequenced movement and tick-stamped state deltas from reliable ordered joins, accepted discrete commands, lifecycle changes, gateway/terminal transitions and grants. An operation ID makes retries idempotent. Reliable messages that depend on a baseline carry its epoch/sequence. Reject stale epochs, request a fresh baseline on gaps and apply only subsequent deltas. Bind authenticated profile, participant and current transport peer separately. Filter **before serialization** by permitted realm, discovered geography, spectator viewpoint and warnable threats. Share the approved party clock/XP/status even across realms. A sealed payload containing a hidden enemy's coordinates still leaks information.

**Responsiveness contract.** On a guest, predict voluntary movement immediately from its own legal sequenced inputs, last confirmed movement stats and known local blockers. On a host snapshot reset to the authoritative pose/tick, drop acknowledged inputs and replay outstanding legal inputs with authoritative statuses and obstacle revision. Smooth only the drawing of small corrections. Snap invalid penetration, major displacement and realm/epoch changes. Forced motion, crowd control, Vault commitment, costs, damage and rewards are authoritative. An optional visual anticipation of an ability cannot grant extra displacement or evade a hit. Interpolate other actors from timestamped samples. Send telegraph origin, geometry, activation and impact times early enough for the tested latency envelope. On a missing baseline resynchronize, record late warnings and test whether counterplay remained possible. Avoid client-specific extensions to a damaging window. ### Native session security (F-15)

Trust only an explicitly invited participant. Bind loopback for local automation and a selected private interface for authorized LAN/direct-IP play. Use bounded, versioned primitive messages. Validate length, count, numeric range, sender, allowed command, sequence, epoch and recipient before allocation or dispatch. Bound both per-peer command rates and decoding work. Reject duplicate/illegal spends, unauthorized ownership and abusive traffic while preserving an honest peer's progress. Never deserialize arbitrary engine objects from peers. No automatic port mapping, public bind, firewall opening or Internet exposure is authorized by this specification.

Raw ENet does not provide confidentiality. For sensitive sessions and reclaimable credentials, propose Godot's native ENet DTLS extension with authenticated server identity. Set up server DTLS immediately after binding and client DTLS before connecting. Verify hostname against a locally trusted certificate, with an explicitly paired fingerprint/trust anchor for direct IP. The host separately authenticates the invited participant through a challenge over this channel. Bind a high-entropy reconnect proof to participant/run/epoch, rotate on use and reject stale/competing controllers. Keep private keys/tokens out of logs and ordinary network snapshots. Require a protected authenticated channel for live credentials and reward reconciliation. Plain loopback tests may use disposable identities. On certificate or capability failure close the protected session. A TLS-protected WebSocket adapter could reuse the protocol at the cost of ordered-stream latency. External transport or identity services would require separate approval. Test certificate mismatch, replay, malformed/oversized input and real exported peers before making a security claim. [Godot ENetConnection](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/classes/class_enetconnection.html) · [Godot TLSOptions](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/classes/class_tlsoptions.html)

**Progression and participation.** Lock scaling party size at run initialization. Keep shared XP/level and applicable geography in the session. Generate and freeze each player's reward independently. Give the encounter a stable ID and track presence by realm, encounter, time and participant. Record late nearby arrival with zero damage as eligible for the confirmed case. A remotely absent player keeps shared XP without receiving that local blessing. Kill, assist, local entitlement, personal mastery and party-wide final victory have separate predicates. Enforce personal wallets, purchases and bargain consent. PR05 defines the prototype radius and exit/death/disconnect cases. Shared challenge access follows its own eligibility and cannot grant permanent ownership. [GDS §§5, 20–23](GDS.md#gds-coop)

**Death and connectivity.** Store body location, cumulative revive waits, retained channel progress, participants, interruption reason, partial revived HP and protection on the authority. Rescue requires real access to the corpse, including across realms. Additional rescuers share one diminishing-return meter. Dead players may spectate the corpse/an eligible living teammate and choose frozen rewards. Disconnect releases movement input. The character stays stationary at its actual position, vulnerable, and committed effects follow their lifecycles. A living disconnected character counts toward survival only within finite grace. Grace expiry changes wipe eligibility, not physical HP. Reconnect to authoritative living or dead state, pending rewards, timers and world epoch. Use PR05–PR07 for prototype eligibility, the ordinary guest clock, reconnect and the tested party envelope.

### Original-host recovery (OWNER-T01, F-03)

Only the original authority may resume its run. Use original issuer identity and key, a unique run ID, persisted authority generation, exclusive writer ownership and a verified recovery frontier. Proving possession of a reconnect token or a guest snapshot cannot establish host authority. Keep connection maintenance time separate from logical gameplay time.

| Failure | Required behavior and evidence |
|---|---|
| Ordinary guest disconnects | A healthy host continues authoritative play. Guest movement stops, body remains vulnerable and PR06 governs survivor grace. Healthy peers continue. A guest missing host packets suspends its own gameplay view while seeking the authoritative result. |
| Temporary loss of host connectivity | On established host loss, park the authoritative run at a recoverable boundary. Peers display suspended state and keep only recovery traffic. No world time, damage, reward generation or guest simulation advances independently. Loss of one guest link alone cannot pause healthy peers. In a two-peer partition silence cannot identify which endpoint failed. An isolated guest waits. A host that cannot establish its own continuity/reachability parks conservatively. Reconcile to the sole original history. |
| Host process crashes | Surviving peers retain last authority identity, published frontier and first loss time. The host needs durable closed state plus a verified continuation through the externally observed frontier. In-memory state can be lost. |
| Original application restarts | Reopen the original protected store/key, acquire exclusive ownership, invalidate the obsolete generation and prove the same run, rules/content revision, frontier and monotonic recovery deadline. Reconcile participant watermarks, offers, inputs and previously issued awards. Send a fresh permitted baseline and obtain synchronization evidence before reopening commands. |
| Missing state, invalid proof, timeout or terminal decision | Failure to recover safely ends the **interrupted** live run, preserves settled progression and starts outstanding reconciliation. A previously durable terminal decision is replayed once as such. An interrupted run earns no invented victory. A terminal tombstone prevents a later restart from reopening play. |
| Later reward recovery | Apply the original issuer's outstanding authorizations under §6, even after the session ends and when hosting roles reverse. Reward delivery cannot resurrect the run. |

Start the **180 s** real-time return opportunity at the first recorded host-loss declaration. Use an elapsed-time witness that survives the relevant failure (a surviving peer's monotonic clock and retained original expiry where available). A restarted timer, host wall-clock rollback or repeated reconnect cannot extend it. An authenticated original return at or before the deadline receives **up to 30 s from that return** to synchronize. A return at +179 s must complete by +209 s. Latest possible completion is +210 s. On conflicting timing evidence use the earliest verifiable deadline. If timely return, continuity or the remaining interval cannot be proven, fail closed. Pre-suspension ordinary guest grace resumes in logical time after successful resynchronization. Never elect a guest or convert speculative input into progress.

**Required recovery image.** Preserve topology and modifications, active and distant actors, poses, fog/encounters/objectives/stock, world time and tier, party/death/revive/disconnect state, RNG streams and draw positions, individual builds, frozen choices/rotation, costs/cooldowns, offensive snapshots, delivery-local counters, status/barrier/contact intervals, causal waves, accepted inputs, receipt states and terminal/epoch decisions. A periodic consistent checkpoint plus verified ordered changes or replay through the last published boundary is a candidate. Compare state digest and acknowledged frontier against surviving peers. Checkpoint age alone proves nothing. Restore an in-flight group only from a complete cursor/queue/pre-state image, or replay the unexposed group from its verified closed start with identical admissions. Fence irreversible grants by original IDs. If an observed boundary cannot be reconstructed, interrupt. Benchmark the checkpoint cost and restart latency inside the approved windows. ## 6. Durable progression

### Settlement classes (OWNER-T03)

The profile is local-first. A permanent purchase writes debit, ownership and its receipt in one transaction. An earned personal discovery is distinct from its later purchase or run acquisition. Each grant records scope, source and recipient. The durable record of a regional blessing or Hunter selection preserves the award and recovery state for **that run**. Its temporary benefit does not become a permanent stat.

| Class | Commitment boundary and player-visible guarantee |
|---|---|
| Important discrete progression | Discoveries, unlocks, personal recipe verification, permanent purchases, significant quest rewards, regional boss and Hunter settlements, victory and other declared important grants each require an immediate durable transaction at adjudication. Do not wait for the currency timer. Before exposing an important event as earned, atomically persist the adjudicating occurrence or replayable cause, its one-time consumption, award and recovery frontier. Gate publication of the affected group and conflicting dependent choices on successful commit. If interrupted before commit, retain the last closed state and enough durable admission evidence to replay any already accepted consequential action without losing an earned important entitlement. In co-op, issuance on the host and application on the recipient have separate indicators. Transport delivery or an in-memory balance cannot establish either. |
| High-frequency ordinary enemy currency only | Accumulate by eligible recipient and stable source occurrence, with a **provisional maximum of 5 seconds** of eligible earnings since the oldest pending item, using monotonic elapsed time during active play. Start at the first pending earning. Schedule writes early enough to meet the limit. Track oldest age and amount across queued and in-flight batches. Dispatch alone cannot restart the clock. At the deadline, fence additional earnings until the oldest batch is committed. Five seconds bounds loss exposure while permitting multiple small drops per transaction. Select a shorter interval if native measurements support it. |
| Mandatory flushes | Flush pending ordinary earnings on graceful quit, ordinary defeat, victory, final-gateway transition, orderly host suspension and any important settlement sharing the recipient's pending funds. Before a permanent purchase uses them, commit them in that transaction or first settle them. A successful ordinary defeat/graceful end retains **all** eligible earnings. |
| Failed or slow storage | Upon missed deadline, full disk or failed important write, fence dependent progression and further accumulation, retain pending state, expose a storage/retry indication and retry the same operation. Never label pending funds as safely stored or a graceful settlement as complete during failure. The 5 s bound limits *earnings at risk* on an unexpected crash under healthy operation. Record delayed I/O as a fault. Wall time to a successful write cannot be guaranteed on failed hardware. |

### From an earned entitlement to a personal receipt

| State | Evidence and transition |
|---|---|
| Entitlement adjudicated | The authority has established the eligible source, participant and amount/outcome under this rules revision. An in-memory decision is pending. |
| Authority durably issued | Atomically commit the source's claimed entitlement, immutable per-recipient payloads and undelivered outbox entries. Bind terminal awards to the same terminal decision. Solo/local permanent settlement can apply the local receipt in the same transaction. |
| Pending delivery | The issuer retains an authenticated grant envelope until the recipient confirms durable application. Transmission timestamps are operational metadata. |
| Recipient durably applied | Verify issuing identity, original run, recipient, grant contents and scope. Atomically commit the economic change, unlock/verification and unique receipt key/digest. A duplicate returns the original result. Reject a different payload for the same key. |
| Delivery acknowledged | Only after recoverable local commit does the recipient return an authenticated application acknowledgement. The issuer durably records it. Loss of this message triggers harmless redelivery. |
| Outstanding reconciliation | After reconnect, restart, interruption or run end, exchange missing grants/receipts. A later host can transport an old envelope. Only the original issuer's authorization can establish its contents. No timeout cancels a recoverable claim. |

Use a stable key comprising the original issuer's persistent public identity, unique durable run ID, source occurrence/entitlement ID, award kind and recipient profile identity. Generate a new run identity on a new run, preserve it on recovery, and enforce database uniqueness. Reuse deterministic source occurrences on replay, including enemy original identity vs combat life. Individual permanent one-time achievements also have profile-wide uniqueness. Store one receipt key and payload digest per applied award. Out-of-order and missing awards remain individually distinguishable across repeated sessions. No highest-sequence shortcut may discard a hole. After acknowledged delivery, compact bulky transport data while retaining enough issuance/receipt evidence to reject every old replay. Retain undelivered envelopes in full indefinitely. Key storage grows with historical awards. Measure it, batch ordinary currency and compact confirmed payloads without deleting deduplication evidence.

**Reversed roles.** If A issued an award while hosting for B and B later hosts, A's profile can resend the stored envelope as a guest. B verifies the original issuer key paired with the original participant/run, matches its own recipient identity and checks its receipt. If already applied, B returns the original acknowledgement. Otherwise B applies it once and acknowledges. A third-party courier would have no power to change or create it. Persist key provenance, immutable contents and a verifiable issuer signature (or equivalent independently verifiable authenticator) with the grant. Keep private keys local. If the only authoritative record is destroyed before delivery, disclose that the award cannot be reconstructed. Preserve recoverable evidence from either party. A permanently unavailable device can leave delivery outstanding. [Godot 4.7 Crypto](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/classes/class_crypto.html)

### Transaction storage and fault boundaries (F-03)

**Recommended native candidate, subject to material dependency approval.** Use an embedded SQLite store per profile in the local user-data directory. Host checkpoints, terminal records, outbox and the host's own profile transaction share the durability boundary where required. Prefer rollback journal DELETE with synchronous EXTRA for the proposed native configuration. Verify actual mode and successful transaction return, including I/O errors, on the selected binding and filesystem. Use one owner connection in exclusive locking mode, obtain the write lock with a real opening write transaction before admitting play and hold it until profile close. Reject a second active process on the same canonical profile path. Recovery after process death uses the database's lock and journal recovery. A PID file, a timestamp or checking whether a file exists has a race. Each authority/peer in multi-process QA gets a distinct disposable profile and path. [SQLite locking and synchronization modes](https://www.sqlite.org/pragma.html) · [SQLite atomic commit](https://www.sqlite.org/atomiccommit.html)

Keep schema/rules/content versions, profile identity, quantities and immutable keys explicit. Use canonical exact encodings for values beyond native integer/JSON range. Make a consistent backup with the database backup facility or another verified database-consistent copy, migrate a copy, validate integrity, foreign keys, balances and receipt history, and retain the last good generation. Refuse unsupported future schemas and competing writable clones. Restore an old backup only after reconciling newer acknowledged history, or keep it quarantined as incomplete. A checksum can detect corruption but cannot prove freshness or persistence. Separate process-kill evidence from OS crash/power interruption under documented storage assumptions. Destroyed media, an OS that fails to honor flushes or loss of the sole newer record cannot be guaranteed recoverable. [SQLite backup](https://www.sqlite.org/backup.html) · [Godot data paths](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/io/data_paths.html)

**Why change the first draft.** A custom append journal and alternating snapshots require independently proven inter-process exclusion, publication, replay, compaction and recovery. Godot FileAccess/DirAccess alone provide no established cross-process locking and power-loss contract here. A flush, rename or reread is insufficient evidence. SQLite supplies established transaction/locking machinery without an external service. The godot-sqlite GDExtension is an MIT-licensed candidate for Godot 4.x with upstream Windows and browser builds. SQLite core is public domain. There is no recurring hosting fee. Pinning, license notices, native binary provenance, backup support, compatible Godot 4.7 Windows exports, optional Web packaging and updates impose maintenance. The exact binding/build remains unapproved and untested here. No dependency is installed. If it cannot qualify, present an equivalent durability/locking alternative for review before relying on it. Optional account sync remains outside scope. [Binding and platform support](https://github.com/2shady4u/godot-sqlite) · [SQLite licensing](https://www.sqlite.org/copyright.html)

## 7. Numerical range and performance

GDS §23 separates unbounded enemy HP/damage growth from bounded population, reinforcement, speed and control resistance. Preserve the pre-Collapse reference, the first tier at 25:00 and subsequent two-minute boundaries. Update an existing enemy's maximum and future offense while preserving absolute current HP, current barrier capacity, reached phases and committed attacks. New spawns take their current-tier full HP. Run level drives advanced XP cost. Keep survival time, XP, reinforcement budgets, final-arena difficulty and world-tier multiplication distinct. No device target, supported population, party maximum or verified frame rate follows from these formulas. [GDS §§21–23](GDS.md#gds-scaling)

**One coordinate space per physical realm (F-09).** Store authoritative positions in realm-local coordinates. Bake module transforms at generation and use continuous realm-space geometry across seams. Create a new realm origin on actual realm travel. Retain the current representation only where measured spacing at extrema preserves the smallest relevant contact, clearance, TOI order and visual alignment. Standard Godot Vector2 components use 32-bit precision while GDScript scalar floats use 64 bits. If required, use two 64-bit scalar components for authoritative spatial math and project to camera-local render coordinates. Revisit sector origins only after the validated realm envelope fails. [Godot 4.7 coordinate precision](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/physics/large_world_coordinates.html)

**One quantity contract.** HP, barriers, damage, healing and XP use finite, signed exact rational quantities for authoritative comparisons and ledger changes. Currency and integral progression use the denominator-one subset. Use checked native integers/scaled integers for the ordinary exact case. The widened form uses signed, dynamically sized integer numerator and positive denominator in reduced form. Cancel common factors before multiplication/division. IDs remain opaque exact strings/bytes or checked integers with no arithmetic coercion. Snapshot, replication, save and protocol use canonical, versioned encodings of the same value. Example: serialize one third as the reduced integer pair 1 and 3, never through a JSON float. Percentages and the geometric Collapse multipliers can be supplied as exact ratios. For genuinely non-rational source inputs (such as spatial measurements or a nonintegral curve exponent), define and version the conversion at the rule boundary using a validated approximation/error interval. Refine a near-threshold result or use a reference evaluation before making a branch-changing decision. Never convert a positive contribution to zero unnoticed.

Check native additions, products, comparisons and exponentiation **before** overflow or discarded remainder. Promote operands before the failing operation. A compensated floating fast estimate can aid display or broad-phase estimates. A double-double or fixed mantissa/exponent still loses sufficiently tiny changes, so neither is the final authority. For a hit consume eligible layers, compute new HP and record **old HP minus represented new HP**. Healing is represented new HP minus old HP, with caps applied first. These exact deltas control recovery, triggers and terminal equality. Require conservation, monotone changes absent an explicit inverse event and identical round trips. Exercise 10^400 HP taking one unit twice, an exact one-unit heal, layered overkill and repeated fractions. No arbitrary practical damage floor, duration limit or tier ceiling follows. Large numerators/denominators and exact state encoding can be expensive. Profile them, use common fast paths and capture a recoverable explicit resource fault if the machine cannot represent the next operation. Never saturate, wrap, manufacture applied damage or continue with corrupt totals. [GDS scaling](GDS.md#gds-scaling) · [Godot JSON](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/classes/class_json.html)

**Contact completeness.** Measure every gameplay candidate from the [authoritative realm index](#tds-space) against independent geometric enumeration. Godot's result-limited diagnostic queries and cosmetic culling cannot bound gameplay targets. Track candidates, exact contacts, duplicates, rejected shapes, processing age and oracle mismatches.

**Workload and resource lifetime.** Measure combinations of dense crowds, split realms, autonomous faction battles, concurrent projectile sweeps/fields, branching kills, active statuses, pending pickups and reliable peer traffic. Use spatial filtering and scheduled due work. Bound caches, visual histories and diagnostic buffers. Reuse high-churn views or buffers when profiling shows a gain. Fully clear stale timers, signals, hurt lists and owner references on reuse. Keep entity identity independent of pool slot. Unload unused visual resources while retaining consequential world state. Preserve protected tell time, finite authored spawn rules and actual simulation outcomes under load. When a population allowance is full, skip that spawn opportunity without deleting living actors or banking a later flood.

**Measure on the playable build.** Record machine, renderer, resolution, export type, ruleset, seed, peer topology and workload. Sample frame/physics time distributions and worst stalls, authoritative step backlog, contacts/events, memory high-water and post-run retention, network bytes/latency, and input-to-visible feedback. Compare equivalent runs with diagnostics on/off and effects at different settings. Profile physics/scripts, CPU/GPU rendering and network separately. Animated sprites and grouped compatible draws are candidates. MultiMesh can help repeated visuals but culls as a group, so partition by visible region and check draw order. Select budgets from representative native measurements and documented target conditions. Browser sampling is conditional on the compatibility claim being made. [Godot 4.7 Profiler](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/scripting/debug/the_profiler.html) · [Godot 4.7 MultiMesh](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/performance/using_multimesh.html) For the original host separate simulation CPU utilization from snapshot serialization/compression time, bytes per peer/channel, baseline resend cost, guest reconciliation latency and checkpoint/commit duration. Compare one active realm with four split players at comparable density and dense Collapse. Record host/guest frame and input-to-visible distributions. Four is a test envelope under PR07. No count or throughput is a demonstrated capacity.

Inspect scene tree, entity/resource values and debugger log, simulate real input, capture the rendered game and compare before/after state. Use GUT for rule assertions and the Profiler for costs. ### Development-only real-input pilot (F-05)

Use a continuous local controller for supervised normal play. Give it rendered pixels/audio or a restricted observation interface exposing exactly the UI, visibility, fog, disclosed cues and choices available to its own player at that moment. Operate only the normal input route, including GUI focus, remapped actions and target validation. The controller can steer, dodge, explore known routes, interact, rescue, choose legal rewards, and return to preparation without waiting for a model round trip per movement. MCP starts/stops and supervises sessions. Keep privileged state, seed, debugger, traces and future RNG in a one-way evidence collector unavailable to the pilot. Record observation policy/version, timestamped input stream, legal profile origin and external interventions. A controller that reads hidden entities, edits a profile, warps, grants resources or issues internal combat commands invalidates a normal-play claim. A guest pilot obeys ordinary disconnection and host suspension. Exclude its controller and privileged hooks from player exports. For a clean native export use external legal input or supervised player input and a separate capture. Compare with pilot-instrumented runs and disclose overhead. [QAS verification modes](QAS.md#qas-modes)

Keep the player-facing Simple Run Summary to outcome, survival duration, final level, defeats, final build, earned rewards and new unlocks. Contextual Mastery Records have their own approved purpose. Local investigation adds neither required remote telemetry nor a player-facing replay/analytics service. [GDS §§22.2, 25.2](GDS.md#gds-onboarding)
