### Non-blocking observations and retained limits

- **`WP07-O1` (Low / boundary observation for WP08 / WP09):** In `src/spatial/realm_geometry.gd:168-170`, `displace_circle()` calls `commit_circle_pose()` (which clears `shape.motion_end`) only when `result.finish != start`. Confirmed in `adversarial-probe.log` (`staged_motion_retained_after_zero_displacement=true`): if a circle has a staged trajectory (`stage_circle_motion`) and a forced `displace_circle()` is blocked immediately at `t = 0`, the prior `motion_end` remains staged. WP08/WP09 callers intending a zero-distance blocked displacement to cancel staged movement must explicitly clear/recommit pose.
- **`WP07-O2` (Observation / defensive guard):** `RealmCircleTime._binary_parts()` (`src/spatial/realm_circle_time.gd:398`) assumes a strictly positive finite float (currently guaranteed by `_axis_hit` guards `gap.lower > 0.0` and `advance.lower != 0.0`).
- **Retained scope limits:** Synthetic headless crowd probes do not establish live combat/render frame budgets; live movement, dynamic solid routing, rendered tell/collider alignment and sustained combined load remain due in **WP08, WP17, WP32 and WP37**.
