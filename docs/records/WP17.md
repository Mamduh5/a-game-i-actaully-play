- **Blocking findings:**
  - **`WP17-F1` (High — Blocking `WP17` approval, `WP18`, `WP32`): Passive momentum transfer in `WorldMomentum.transfer()` completely ignores `displacement_resistance`.**
    - **Location:** `src/world/world_momentum.gd:178-207`.
    - **Governing rule:** [GDS §16.3](../GDS.md#gds-environment) specifies `DeltaVelocity = DirectionalImpulse / PositiveEffectiveMass * (1 - DisplacementResistance)`, separates mass and forced-displacement resistance, requires passive collisions not to add impact energy, and [WP17](../IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md#wp17) owns resolving directed momentum and resistance.
    - **Reproducible counterexample (`.qa-evidence/WP17-review/adversarial.log`):**
      - Create `mover = WorldMomentum.create(1.0, 0.0)` with impulse `(10.0, 0.0)` (`v_x = 10.0`) and `immune = WorldMomentum.create(1.0, 1.0)` (100% forced-displacement resistance; direct `apply_impulse((100.0, 0.0), ...)` correctly yields `v_x = 0.0`).
      - Calling `mover.contact(&"boss", 0, (1, 0), 0.1, immune)` then `mover.transfer(imp, &"mover", 0, 0.5)` sets **`immune.velocity().x = 7.5`** (`mover.velocity().x = 2.5`), launching a 100%-displacement-resistant body as if its resistance were `0.0`.
      - Similarly, with `resistant = WorldMomentum.create(1.0, 0.9)` (where a direct `10.0` impulse produces `v_x = 1.0`), `mover2.transfer(..., restitution = 0.0)` gives **`resistant.velocity().x = 5.0`** (5× higher displacement than a direct knockback of equal impulse, and `7.5×` at `restitution = 0.5`).
    - **Required correction & retest:** Incorporate forced-displacement resistance into passive momentum transfer (preserving zero displacement at `displacement_resistance = 1.0`, reduced passive displacement at partial resistance, bounded restitution, and non-increasing kinetic energy across all approach combinations including moving resistant bodies). Add positive/negative unit tests covering `0.0`, partial (`0.9`) and full (`1.0`) resistance on both mover and receiver, plus energy bounds, and re-verify on the corrected candidate.
