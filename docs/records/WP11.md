## Implementation

- Maximum HP preserves exact current HP up to the new cap. The change generates no hit, recovery or sacrifice receipt.
- Personal barriers absorb compatible damage in deadline order, using creation order for ties and persistent layers last. Replacement compares whole instances, and capped accumulation preserves the existing layer identity and deadline unless explicitly refreshed. Generation distinguishes subsequent instances with the same source ID. Expiry, replacement, clamp, decay and removal have separate non-hit receipts. A hit due at the deadline gets its full causal group before natural expiry.
- Typed damage-recovery rules consume the hit's attributed hostile HP debit, apply each source's limit and compatible amplification once, and publish restored, eligible excess and discarded amounts. A bound companion uses its controlling life as the recovery recipient. The kernel exposes eligible excess to later named consumers. WP20 and content packages own named forms, storage/conversion behavior and their reciprocal eligibility.
- HP-priced activation offers join the run queue at the committed instant. Authored priority and canonical provenance settle competition before that group's contacts. The provisional key may stage a contact at the payment instant. Future delivery uses a successfully paid key. Combined cost and cooldown settle atomically. The lethal debit uses actual HP, bypasses layers, records the death state and cancels the affected cast without a hit or hostile retaliation. The automatic fatal-price protection interval ends at its declared deadline.
- A distinct controller binding gates new ordinary actions. Authored continuous delivery also checks controller liveness when admitted and when its contact resolves. Already committed independent delivery retains the original provenance and its eligible kill reactions while the run stays active. Terminal evaluation follows the fully closed group. A same-group fortified release can defeat the Creator and future delivery cannot prolong a wipe.

WP22 owns rescue and revival state transitions and uses the activation receipt as a separate death fact. WP36/WP39/WP40 own durable terminal settlement, peer trials and the corresponding named content fixtures.
