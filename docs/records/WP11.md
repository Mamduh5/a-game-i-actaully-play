The lethal debit uses actual HP, bypasses layers, records the death state and cancels the affected cast without a hit or hostile retaliation. The automatic fatal-price protection interval ends at its declared deadline.

Already committed independent delivery retains the original provenance and its eligible kill reactions while the run stays active. Terminal evaluation follows the fully closed group. A same-group fortified release can defeat the Creator and future delivery cannot prolong a wipe.
