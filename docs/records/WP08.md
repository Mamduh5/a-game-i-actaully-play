### Required corrections (blocking WP08 approval)

1. **`WP08-D1` (High · Blocking — `src/controls/player_input_router.gd:113-134`, `dev/fixtures/wp08_controls.gd`):** **Stale GUI keyboard focus hijacks arrow-key movement (`KEY_UP` / `KEY_DOWN`) after clicking exposed ground.**
   - **Governing rule:** [GDS §2](../GDS.md#gds-controls) (*"Left-click movement, arrow-key movement and other player configurations must be supportable"* · *"When the reward side panel is open, movement remains possible on visible ground through the configured movement action"*) and [TDS §8 non-blocking input routing](../TDS.md#tds-input) (*"Focus, UI hit testing and remapping must preserve that separation"* · *"Verify mouse-first, swapped bindings, keyboard movement plus click, keyboard focus activation and drag-across interactions"*).
   - **Expected:** Clicking a panel button focuses it for keyboard activation (`Space`/`Enter`); clicking back onto exposed world ground returns focus to the world so configured arrow-key movement (`KEY_UP`, `KEY_DOWN`, `KEY_LEFT`, `KEY_RIGHT`) moves the player instead of cycling panel buttons (and `Space` no longer triggers stale button clicks after returning to world control).
   - **Actual:** Clicking `Swap mouse and use arrows` leaves GUI focus on `@Button@6` even after subsequent clicks on exposed ground `(560, 395)` and `(505, 340)` (visible in both writer `wp08-final.png` and reviewer `wp08-review-swapped.png`). Holding `KEY_UP` produces zero movement (`pos` remains `[560, 395]`, `up_arrow_cmd_count=0`) because Godot's GUI consumes `ui_up` to shift focus to `FixtureReward`; subsequently holding `KEY_DOWN` also produces zero movement (`pos` remains `[560, 395]`) because Godot's GUI consumes `ui_down` to shift focus back to `@Button@6`. Writer's MCP run missed this only because it tested `KEY_DOWN` while focus sat on the bottom button with no focus neighbor below.
   - **Reproduction:** Launch `res://dev/fixtures/wp08_controls.tscn` → click `Swap mouse and use arrows` `(1000, 282)` → left-click exposed ground `(560, 395)` → press and hold `KEY_UP` (15 ticks) → press and hold `KEY_DOWN` (15 ticks). Observed in `.qa-evidence/WP08-review/rendered-verify.json` and `.qa-evidence/WP08-review/adversarial-probe.log` (`after_ground_click_b2_focused=true up_arrow_cmd_count=0 b1_now_focused=true`).
   - **Required fix:** Release stale GUI focus when an unhandled world pointer press is accepted on exposed ground (preserving button focus after GUI clicks so `Space` still activates a freshly clicked reward button), and add opposing unit and rendered/MCP controls covering `KEY_UP` and `KEY_DOWN` after swapping and clicking exposed ground.

2. **`WP08-D2` (Medium · Blocking — `src/controls/player_control_state.gd:40-67`):** **`_destination` is never cleared upon arrival, causing involuntary walk-back after forced displacement (knockback).**
   - **Governing rule:** [GDS §2.1](../GDS.md#gds-controls) & [WP08/WP12 contract](../IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md#wp08): voluntary movement and forced displacement are distinct; completing a click-to-move destination must not leave a stale target that automatically pulls the actor back after a later knockback.
   - **Expected:** Once `PlayerControlState.step()` reaches `_destination`, `_destination` clears (`null`) so a later `RealmGeometry.displace_circle()` knockback leaves the actor at the displaced position until new voluntary input arrives. Unreached destinations interrupted by a temporary blocker (e.g., a door broken on a subsequent tick) still resume until reached.
   - **Actual:** After arriving at `(10, 0)` from `MOVE_TO(10, 0)`, displacing the circle back to `(0, 0)` and calling `step(..., 100.0, 0.05)` with zero new commands walks the actor back to `x = 5.0` (`.qa-evidence/WP08-review/adversarial-probe.log`: `post_arrival_knockback_walkback_x=5.0`).
   - **Blocking scope:** Blocks WP12 (guest prediction/reconciliation under forced motion) and WP17 (forced displacement).
   - **Required fix:** Clear `_destination` in `PlayerControlState.step()` once the committed pose reaches `_destination` (while preserving `_destination` when movement stops short at a blocker), and add a unit regression covering arrival → knockback → stationary next step alongside the existing blocked-door test.

### Non-blocking hardening finding (recommended in the same revision)

- **`WP08-F3` (Low — `src/controls/player_input_router.gd:52-64, 80-81`):** `rebind()` checks binding conflicts using only `InputEvent.is_match()`. Because `_supported_binding()` accepts both `physical_keycode` and `keycode`, rebinding `RIGHT` to `InputEventKey(keycode=KEY_A, physical_keycode=KEY_NONE)` succeeds against default `LEFT` (`physical_keycode=KEY_A, keycode=KEY_NONE`) (`adversarial-probe.log`: `rebind_logical_A_against_physical_A_allowed=true`), allowing two actions on the same physical key `A`. Compare normalized `_key_id()` equality for `InputEventKey` pairs alongside `is_match()`.
