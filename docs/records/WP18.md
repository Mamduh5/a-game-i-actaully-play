## Full-step baseline

- A host steps every registered realm on the same next logical tick. The step drains strictly earlier combat first, processes scheduled population and decisions, and releases the exact closed groups. It can retain an open tick across storage wait. The host evidence image and viewport projections query the closed result. Skipping a tick is rejected without consuming work or RNG.
- Factions select their authored hostiles through actual realm geometry. A Hunter prioritizes a sensed living player, retains its last seen point within the configured time and can engage an opposing faction after losing pursuit. Pursuit uses the navigable graph and a certified sweep for the actor's own size. Named archetype behavior and authored numeric tuning belong to the catalog packages. No new game-wide tuning is inferred from the fixture.
- Committed strikes retain their windup location and paid delivery. Moving outside the contact before release avoids damage. A reanimated enemy retains its entity identity and incremented combat life, clears previous statuses and barriers and uses its authored recovery. Stale contacts for the old life do not pay damage or elimination. A further actual player kill keeps player provenance. Farm limits and authored XP delivery remain later integrated witnesses.
- Scheduled spawn opportunities use named seeded streams. An unvisited registered realm can spawn at a due tick. A full site spends the opportunity and carries no backlog. Reanimation leaves site identity available to later capacity checks. Limits for a site's new spawn opportunities remain distinct from full-world population pressure.
