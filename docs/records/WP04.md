- **`WP04-R1` (Empirical Risk for WP09 / WP27 / WP37):** GDScript `RefCounted` wrapper allocation (~`82,780 Quantity` + `7,785 QuantityFraction` per 96 histories, ~`65–160 µs` per mixed-rate posting, ~250× over bare int loops) is acceptable for WP04 adoption and early M2/M3 integration, but WP37 must measure numeric CPU share under sustained crowded Collapse combat + presentation.
