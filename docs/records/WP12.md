#### Findings required before final WP12 approval

1. **`WP12-F1` (Medium · Required before final WP12 approval — `src/replication/guest_movement.gd:63-66, 109-115`):** **Pre-lock unacknowledged voluntary inputs survive `permits_prediction = false` (Vault / crowd control / forced motion) and replay + retransmit once prediction resumes.**
   - **Governing rule:** [TDS §5 Responsiveness contract](../TDS.md#tds-network) (*"Forced motion, crowd control, Vault commitment, costs, damage and rewards are authoritative."*).
   - **Observed (`adversarial-probe.log`):** Issuing `MOVE_AXES(1, 0)` (`seq=1`), receiving `STATE(ack=0, pos=9.0, permits_prediction=false, forced_correction=true)` (where `issue(MOVE_AXES(0, 0))` is rejected), and later receiving `STATE(ack=0, pos=9.0, movement=MOVE_AXES(0, 0), permits_prediction=true)` leaves `g_lock_pending_during_stun=1`, causes `latest_packet()` to retransmit stale `seq=1` (`g_lock_stale_packet_resent_after_stun=true`), and causes `advance()` to walk involuntarily to `x = 10.0` (`g_lock_post_stun_involuntary_x=10.0`).
   - **Required fix:** Clear `_pending` when an authoritative motion lock (`not state.permits_prediction`) or `forced_correction` supersedes unacknowledged voluntary movement, and cover stun/Vault entry → key release → unlock in unit and live Vault/CC tests.

2. **`WP12-F2` (Medium · Required before final WP12 approval — `src/replication/host_movement_bridge.gd:18-42`):** **Same-generation connection reclaim is rejected without `unbind()`, while `unbind()` erases per-seat monotonic `generation`/`epoch`/`baseline` history.**
   - **Observed (`adversarial-probe.log`):** (a) `bridge_same_gen_reclaim_without_unbind_allowed=false` — `NativeSessionHost` keeps `_generation` constant across a host session and live reclaim replaces `old_connection` with a newer `connection` at the same `generation` without reporting `old_connection` in `batch.lost_seats`, yet line 24 rejects `(generation == old.generation and connection != old.connection)`; (b) `bridge_post_unbind_stale_rebind_allowed=true` — calling `unbind(1, 10)` erases `_seats[1]`, allowing a subsequent stale `bind(1, 9, 6, ..., epoch=1, baseline=1)` to succeed.
   - **Required fix:** Retain a per-seat monotonic watermark across `unbind()` (clearing only the active connection mapping) and permit same-generation reclaim for strictly newer admitted connections (`connection > old.connection`) with a strictly newer baseline (or higher epoch) while rejecting stale connections and any regression in `generation`/`epoch`/`baseline`.
