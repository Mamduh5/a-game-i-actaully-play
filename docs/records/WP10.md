An interval that cannot pay ends its channel while already admitted contacts remain active. These entry points accept immediate resource/cooldown commitments. The separate WP11 HP-price offers remain behind their review gate.

It returns the blocker proof and a geometry revision so the run can deliberately handle shield durability and retire the delivery. Resolving consumable geometry across changing revisions must use a fresh query.

An equal-deadline tick settles before natural expiry, while destruction, replacement, cleanup and refused interval payment have distinct ends. Even a missed tick advances cadence. A separate per-target life ledger survives absence and re-entry and advances only when a certified contact is admitted.
